The paper and your 150 minutes

Before any skill, know the ground. The paper has the same shape every year, so you can decide today how you will spend the time.

On the paperTwo parts, 20 marks, 2½ hours

Part One: Reading Comprehension (Score: 12/20) … Part Two: Writing (Choose ONE of the two prompts below.) (Score: 08/20)Every LS and SE paper since 2016. Duration: ساعتان ونصف, two hours and a half.

Part One is built on one selection of about 600–800 words with a short preview line in italics. Its questions come in lettered sections. The letters move a little from year to year (false statements have been B, D and E), but the kinds of questions do not. Tap each one:

The paper, section by section

How it’s markedPoint by point

In the official grading schemes, reading answers earn marks per idea, usually 0.5 each. A 1-mark question almost always wants two ideas. The essay is marked on three things: content and organisation 3.5, language and style 3.5, tidiness and legible handwriting 1.

The methodPlan the 150 minutes before exam day

Drag to choose how long you give to reading

Part One (reading): 75 minPart Two (writing): 60 minFinal check: 15 min
  1. Skim the text and its preview line (5 min).
  2. Read every question; underline command words and paragraph numbers (5 min).
  3. Quick marks first: false statements, reference words, vocabulary (15 min).
  4. Written answers of Sections A and C, paragraph by paragraph (35 min).
  5. The table or graph question (10 min), then the essay: plan, write, check.
Many students do the essay first, while they are fresh. That works too, if you stop at your planned time.
