Tone and mood

Tone is the writer’s attitude; mood is the atmosphere a place or scene creates. Name the tone with precise adjectives, then prove each one with the writer’s words.

On the paperReal questions

What two different adjectives best describe the writer’s tone in Paragraph 8? Justify your answer. (01)LS, June 2019
Determine the tone of Jan Kowalzig in Paragraph 6. Justify your answer. (Score: 01pt)LS paper

How it’s markedThe official split

0.5 for each tone with its justification. The official answer (June 2019): The writer’s tone is surprised/pleased/proud and hopeful/wishful. She is surprised by her daughter’s brave reaction… In addition, she reveals the wish of her daughter to have a cure. Several adjectives are accepted if the justification fits.

The methodHow to answer

FamilyTone adjectives

Ironicalsarcastic, critical, cynical, scornful, witty, humorous

Neutralobjective, informative, instructive, serious, rational, impartial (no stand taken)

Clues to tone: the subject, the choice of words and their connotations, the point of view and the purpose. Mood is named with nouns: tranquillity, fear, hope, sadness.

Answer frame: The writer’s tone is [adjective], as she … (“quote”). It is also [adjective] since … (“quote”).

See it doneWorked answers

Question. What two adjectives best describe the writer’s tone in paragraph 7 of Wired Awake? Justify.
The writer’s tone is hopeful, as he opens with “Fortunately, small changes can make a large difference.” It is also instructive, since he lists practical steps for families and schools, such as switching off screens an hour before bed.

0.5 per adjective with its evidence.

Question. What two adjectives best describe the writer’s tone in paragraph 6 of The Price of a Click? Justify.
The tone is ironic, as the writer points out that this generation has more information than any before it “yet it may be less informed”. It is also critical, since he shows that endless headlines give only “the feeling of knowing” while the full story stays unread.

0.5 per adjective with its evidence.

Texts: Wired Awake (LS) and The Price of a Click (SE), in the reading room.
