Answering in your own words

Section A is the biggest block of reading marks. The questions are often easy; the marks are lost in how the answer is written.

On the paperThe instruction, and how it varies

A. Answer each of the following questions in 1-4 complete sentences using your own words.LS and SE papers, 2017–2019
A. In 2-4 sentences, answer the following in your own words.Earlier LS papers. Sometimes “1–3 sentences”: always read the number.

How it’s markedOne idea, half a mark

In the official grading schemes, a (01) question is split into two ideas: 0.5 for each. Reasonable answers in other words are accepted (“any other reasonable answer is acceptable”). An answer copied line by line from the text, or a single idea stretched over four sentences, loses marks.

See it doneThe official answer to a real question

Question (LS, June 2019): Based on Paragraph 1, identify two reasons behind the mother’s sadness. (01)
First, the mother was sad because she knew that her 10-year-old daughter had Diabetes 1. Second, she did not have enough information about Diabetes 1.

0.5 for each says the grading scheme. Notice the shape: it echoes the question (“the mother was sad because”), numbers the ideas (First… Second…), uses complete sentences, and stops when the two ideas are given.

The methodFour habits

  1. Echo the question in your first words, so the answer stands alone: “The first reason was…”.
  2. Count the marks. (01) usually means two ideas. Plan them: first… second…
  3. Rephrase: change the words and the structure, keep the meaning. Names, numbers and technical terms can stay.
  4. Stop when the ideas are given. Four sentences is a maximum, not a target.
