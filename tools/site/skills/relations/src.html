Thematic relations and cohesion

Two questions live here: how two paragraphs are related in meaning, and which words tie them together.

On the paperReal questions

Identify the cohesive device used to link each pair of paragraphs below and specify its type. (01) a. Paragraphs 5 and 6 b. Paragraphs 7 and 8LS, June 2018

How it’s markedThe official split

For the 2018 cohesion question: 0.25 for the device and 0.25 for its type per pair. Official answer: Paragraphs 5 and 6 are linked with the transition signal “In addition”, which is a direct cohesive device. Or: with the repetition of the key terms such as “biological intelligence”; it is an indirect cohesive device.

The methodHow to answer

Thematic relations between paragraphs:

RelationThe second paragraph…
Problem – solutionsolves the problem the first presents
Problem – elaborationdevelops the problem in more detail
Statement – reinforcementconfirms or restates the first idea
Statement – illustrationgives examples of it
Statement – elaborationexplains it in detail
Statement – supportgives evidence: facts, statistics, a live example
Statement – additionadds a new point
Contradictionpresents an opposing idea
Past – presentcompares then and now
Question – answeranswers the question
Cause – effectgives the result of what came before

Cohesive devices: direct links are connectors (moreover, however, as a result, in addition). Indirect links are pronouns, synonyms and repetition of key words. Writers use indirect links so the style does not become heavy with connectors.

See it doneWorked answers

Question. What is the thematic relationship between paragraphs 6 and 7 of Wired Awake? What links them?
The relationship is problem – solution. Paragraph 6 shows how lack of sleep harms teenagers’ mood and health and traps them in a cycle, while paragraph 7 offers ways out: screens off an hour before bed, phones outside the bedroom, lessons on sleep. The link is direct: the connector “Fortunately”.

Name the relation, show the content of both paragraphs, name the link.

Question. What is the thematic relationship between paragraphs 2 and 3 of The Price of a Click?
The relationship is statement – elaboration. Paragraph 2 states that false stories spread because young people rarely check their sources; paragraph 3 explains in detail one reason behind it, the algorithms that push alarming posts to the top. The link is indirect: the repetition of “problem” and the idea of what we see online.

Relation + content of both paragraphs + the link.

Texts: Wired Awake (LS) and The Price of a Click (SE), in the reading room.
