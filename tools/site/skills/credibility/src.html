Credibility and evidence

Why should the reader believe the writer? Because of the evidence. Name each type, and give the example from the text with its paragraph.

On the paperReal questions

Identify two types of evidence the writer uses to achieve credibility. Support your answer with examples. (01)LS, June 2019
State four types of evidence (with examples) that reveal the writer’s credibility in the selection above. (Score: 01pt)LS paper

How it’s markedThe official split

0.5 for each type with its examples. Official answer (June 2019): First, the writer uses numbers as in Paragraphs 2 and 4: “400,000 people suffer from it”… Second, she uses specific names as in Paragraph 9: “House of Parliament”… Any other two types with examples were accepted.

The methodHow to answer

Type of evidenceExample of what to quote
Statistics, numbers“only one in five”, “70 percent”
Study or research results“a recent survey of 1,200 students”
Expert opinion, quotation“…explains Dr Maya Haddad, a sleep researcher”
Live examplea real person’s story: Karim, Rana
Facts: names, dates, places, events“Saida”, “Tripoli”, an organisation’s name
Scientific explanationhow melatonin works
Answer frame: To achieve credibility, the writer uses different types of evidence. First, … as in paragraph … (“…”). Second, … as in paragraph … (“…”).

See it doneWorked answers

Question. Identify two types of evidence the writer of Wired Awake uses to achieve credibility. Give examples.
To achieve credibility, the writer uses different types of evidence. First, he gives the results of a survey in paragraph 2: of 1,200 students, “only one in five” sleeps eight hours. Second, he quotes an expert in paragraph 4, Dr Maya Haddad, a sleep researcher, who compares apps to “a slot machine”.

0.5 per type with its example.

Question. Identify two types of evidence the writer of The Price of a Click uses to achieve credibility. Give examples.
First, the writer gives statistics in paragraph 2: three out of four young adults get their news from social media. Second, he quotes an expert in paragraph 4, Ziad Khoury, a journalism lecturer: “When attention becomes the product, accuracy becomes optional.”

0.5 per type with its example.

Texts: Wired Awake (LS) and The Price of a Click (SE), in the reading room.
