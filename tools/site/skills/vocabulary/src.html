Meaning from context

You will meet words you do not know. The sentence around them almost always tells you what they mean.

On the paperA box, and sentences with blanks

E. Based on contextual clues, infer the meaning of each word in the box below. Then fill in the blanks with the correct words to complete the sentences that follow. The words are underlined in the selection.LS, June 2019, (01). Other years: “Use contextual clues to figure out the meaning of each word…”, or “Give the meaning of the following words using contextual clues.”

How it’s markedRight word, right spelling

Each blank is worth 0.25. The grading scheme adds: N.B.: The spelling of words must be correct. A misspelt right word scores zero. The box usually holds one more word than there are blanks.

The methodFive clues, three checks

ClueSignalExample
Definitionis, means, called, a comma or dashmelatonin, the hormone that makes us sleepy
Synonymor, also, a repeated ideaanxious, nervous
Antonym / contrastbut, unlike, however, whilecalm facts, unlike alarming posts
Examplesuch as, like, includingsymptoms such as thirst, hunger
General sensethe whole situationheads on desks in the first period
  1. Grammar: what does the blank need? After the, a noun; after very, an adjective; after to, a verb.
  2. Meaning: reread the word in its paragraph, then test it in the sentence.
  3. Spelling: copy it letter by letter from the box.
