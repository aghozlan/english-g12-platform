How it’s markedThe official split

Audience (June 2019): 0.5 for each audience with its interest; any other reasonable type with justification is acceptable. The official answer named parents and family members of patients (to learn the symptoms) and health organisations (the obstacles to a cure).

Audience: the general reader reads for interest; a specific audience has a reason, which you must state. Name a precise group (parents of teenagers, school principals, health ministers) and the interest it finds.

See it doneWorked answers

Question. What two types of audience, other than the general reader, might be interested in Wired Awake? State the interest of each.
First, parents of teenagers might be interested, since the selection warns them of how phones damage their children’s sleep and gives them practical rules, like the phone basket. Second, school principals and teachers might be interested, because it explains why students fall asleep in class and suggests teaching how sleep works.

0.5 per audience + interest.

Question. What is the writer’s purpose in The Price of a Click? Explain.
The writer’s purpose is to inform and to persuade. He informs readers about how false news spreads, through a survey, algorithms and advertising money, and then persuades them to read the media more wisely, giving three questions to ask before sharing.

Purpose + how the text achieves it.

Texts: Wired Awake (LS) and The Price of a Click (SE), in the reading room.
