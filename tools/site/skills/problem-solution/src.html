Problem-solution essay

First make the reader see the problem clearly, then offer solutions that are practical: who does what, and how.

On the paperReal prompts

Countries with high levels of gender inequality have lower economic growth. Some studies show that countries with fewer female leaders ignore a wider range of issues such as health, education, and child support. Write a problem-solution essay of 250-300 words in which you identify two different problems (social, economic, political, etc.) caused by gender inequality, and then suggest practical solutions to these problems.SE, August 2018

How it’s markedContent 3.5, language 3.5, tidiness 1

Read the counts: two different problems, practical solutions. The examiner checks that you named two problems, that they really are different (social and economic, for example), and that each solution could actually be done.

The outlineFour or five paragraphs, each with one job

  1. Introduction
    • Hook and background: present the problem
    • Thesis: the problem can be solved
  2. Body 1: the problem
    • Problem (or effect) 1, explained
    • Problem (or effect) 2, explained
  3. Body 2: solutions for citizens
    • What individuals, families or employers can do
  4. Body 3: solutions for the government
    • Laws, services, funding, programmes
  5. Conclusion
    • Restate the problem and the solutions
    • Final thought: what should have been done, or what will follow

The generic form from the course notes. No official outline has been published for this genre.

TechniqueMake a solution practical

  1. Say who acts: parents, employers, schools, the ministry.
  2. Say what they do, concretely: enforce equal pay for equal work, not make things fair.
  3. Link it to the problem it solves: affordable childcare, so that mothers do not have to choose…
