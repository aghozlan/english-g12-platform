A. Answer each question in 1–4 complete sentences using your own words.

1.Based on paragraph 2, identify two reasons that led Hiba to start the seed library.(01)
First, she saw that local farmers were paying a lot for foreign seeds every season. Second, she wanted to revive the old custom of keeping the best seeds and sharing them with neighbours.0.5 for each.
2.Scan paragraph 3 for two rules that members of the library follow.(01)
Members borrow seeds in spring, and after the harvest they must give back double the amount they borrowed.0.5 for each rule. Accept: each packet comes with a card describing the plant.
3.Based on paragraph 4, explain two advantages of local seeds.(01)
Since local seeds have adjusted to the region for centuries, they use less water and fewer chemicals, which matters as summers become hotter. Also, farmers who keep their own seeds are no longer controlled by foreign prices.0.5 for each advantage.
4.Based on paragraph 5, infer how Jad’s view of farming has changed.(01)
Jad used to see farming as an old-fashioned activity for older people. Through the library, he now sees it as important for his own generation’s future.0.5 for the old view, 0.5 for the new one.
