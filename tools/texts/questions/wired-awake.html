A. Answer each of the following questions in 1–4 complete sentences using your own words.

1.Based on paragraph 1, identify two signs that Karim’s night is not a healthy one.(01)
First, Karim is still awake at 1:40 a.m. although he has school the next morning. Second, he keeps promising himself to watch only one more video but does not stop.0.5 for each sign. Accept: he has a test he has not slept enough for.
2.Scan paragraphs 3 and 4 for two reasons teenagers find it hard to fall asleep early.(01)
The first reason is biological: a teenager’s body produces the sleep hormone about two hours later than a child’s, and screen light delays it even more. The second is the design of apps, which keep users watching and checking because rewards come at unexpected moments.0.5 for each reason.
3.Based on paragraphs 5 and 6, explain how lack of sleep affects students’ school life.(01)
Because the brain stores new learning during deep sleep, tired students cannot focus in class and forget much of what they revised. In addition, they become anxious and bad-tempered, and some depend on energy drinks, which keeps them awake again the next night.0.5 for the effect on learning, 0.5 for the effect on mood or health.
4.Based on paragraphs 7 and 8, infer one measure a family and one measure a school could take to protect teenagers’ sleep.(01)
A family could agree that all phones, including the parents’, are left outside the bedrooms at a fixed time every night. A school could hold sessions that explain how sleep affects memory, so that students choose to sleep instead of being ordered to.0.5 for each measure. Any reasonable measure linked to the paragraphs is accepted.
