Command words

The first word of a question is an instruction. Read it wrong and a perfect answer to a different question earns nothing.

On the paperReal question openings

Based on Paragraph 1, identify two reasons behind the mother’s sadness.LS, June 2019
Based on Paragraphs 8 and 9, infer one medical and one governmental measure that could reduce the suffering of children with Diabetes 1.LS, June 2019
Based on Paragraphs 5 and 6, how did Diabetes 1 change the life of Lynn and that of her mother? Explain.LS, June 2019

The methodWhat each word wants

Command wordWhat the examiner wants
IdentifyShow who or what something is: name the idea, reason or person. Short and exact.
Define, list, locateDefine: what a term means and how it differs from similar things. List: related points that start with the same part of speech. Locate: where it is in the text.
Describe, explain, discuss, clarifyDescribe: a picture in words. Explain: make clear and give reasons, especially cause and effect or steps in order.
InferState a meaning the writer suggests but does not say. The writer implies; you infer.
Analyse, evaluateAnalyse: examine the details and the links between them to reach a conclusion. Evaluate: judge the value of something, its pluses and minuses, with evidence.

Command words also tell you how deep to read:

DepthWhat you do
Literal: on the linesFind what is stated: facts, sequences, stated causes, characters, setting.
Inferential: between the linesBuild what is suggested: implied causes, attitude, a word’s meaning from context, a pronoun’s referent.
Critical: beyond the linesJudge the text: its evidence, facts against opinions, whether the argument holds.

Identify

Name who or what: the idea, reason or person. Short, exact.

Explain

Give reasons: cause and effect, or steps in order. Use because, so, as a result.

Infer

Clue in the text + what you know = a meaning the writer suggests. State it clearly.

Evaluate

Judge the value: pluses and minuses, each backed by evidence.
