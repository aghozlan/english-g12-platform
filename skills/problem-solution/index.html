Problem-solution essay

First make the reader see the problem clearly, then offer solutions that are practical: who does what, and how.

On the paperReal prompts

Countries with high levels of gender inequality have lower economic growth. Some studies show that countries with fewer female leaders ignore a wider range of issues such as health, education, and child support. Write a problem-solution essay of 250-300 words in which you identify two different problems (social, economic, political, etc.) caused by gender inequality, and then suggest practical solutions to these problems.SE, August 2018

How it’s markedContent 3.5, language 3.5, tidiness 1

Read the counts: two different problems, practical solutions. The examiner checks that you named two problems, that they really are different (social and economic, for example), and that each solution could actually be done.

The outlineFour or five paragraphs, each with one job

  1. Introduction
    • Hook and background: present the problem
    • Thesis: the problem can be solved
  2. Body 1: the problem
    • Problem (or effect) 1, explained
    • Problem (or effect) 2, explained
  3. Body 2: solutions for citizens
    • What individuals, families or employers can do
  4. Body 3: solutions for the government
    • Laws, services, funding, programmes
  5. Conclusion
    • Restate the problem and the solutions
    • Final thought: what should have been done, or what will follow

The generic form from the course notes. No official outline has been published for this genre.

When half a country is held back

Model essay written for this platform, 265 words.

In many countries, women make up half of the population but only a small share of decision-makers. This gap is not only unfair; it is costly. Gender inequality creates serious economic and social problems, but practical steps taken by both citizens and governments can solve them.

The first problem is economic. When women are kept out of good jobs or paid less for the same work, a country loses the talent of half its workforce, and its growth slows down. The second problem is social. When few women hold leadership positions, issues such as health, education and child support receive less attention. As a result, families suffer, and children grow up with fewer opportunities.

Citizens can begin to change this situation at home and at work. Parents can give their daughters the same education and encouragement they give their sons. Employers can promote people according to their skills, not their gender, and offer flexible hours to working mothers. Moreover, men and women alike can vote for qualified female candidates and support them once they are elected.

The government, for its part, must protect equality through law. It can enforce equal pay for equal work and punish discrimination. It can also provide affordable childcare, so that mothers do not have to choose between a career and their children. Finally, scholarships can prepare more women for leadership.

In conclusion, gender inequality weakens both the economy and society, but it is not a fate we must accept. If families, employers and the state work together, women will contribute fully, and the whole country will share in their success.

TechniqueMake a solution practical

  1. Say who acts: parents, employers, schools, the ministry.
  2. Say what they do, concretely: enforce equal pay for equal work, not make things fair.
  3. Link it to the problem it solves: affordable childcare, so that mothers do not have to choose…
