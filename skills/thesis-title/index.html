Thesis statement and title

Every selection has one main idea. Sometimes a sentence states it; sometimes you must build it from the topic sentences. The title is usually a squeezed version of it.

On the paperReal questions

Which sentence in Paragraph one serves as the thesis statement? Justify your answer. (Score: 1½)LS paper, older session
How is the title related to the main idea of the article? Explain. (Score: 01½)LS paper, older session
Identify the figure of speech used in the title, and show the function it serves. (01)LS paper, 2019

How it’s markedThe official split

Identification and justification are marked separately: 0.5 for naming the sentence (or the figure in the title), 0.5 for explaining why. A justification links the sentence to the body: “…an idea fully developed in the body paragraphs”.

The methodHow to answer

What to look for
Stated thesisOne sentence, usually the last (sometimes the first) of the introduction, that gives the writer’s central point: what the reader should know or believe by the end.
Implied thesisNo single sentence says it. Read the topic sentences of the body and the conclusion, then write the thesis yourself in one sentence.
A real thesisis a complete sentence, gives an opinion or idea rather than a bare fact, and is never a question.
The titlecondenses the thesis. It may do so directly, or through a figure of speech or irony. Explain both what it says and what it means.
Answer frame: The last sentence of the introductory paragraph serves as the thesis statement since it introduces the main idea of the text, which is about… Such an idea is fully elaborated in the body paragraphs.

See it doneWorked answers

Question. Which sentence in paragraph 1 of Wired Awake serves as the thesis statement? Justify.
The last sentence of paragraph 1, “Karim is not lazy, and he is not alone: he belongs to a generation whose nights have quietly been taken over by a screen,” serves as the thesis. It moves from one teenager to a whole generation and states the main idea: screens are taking over teenagers’ sleep. The body develops this idea through its causes (paragraphs 3–4), its effects (5–6) and its solutions (7–8).

0.5 identification, 0.5 justification linked to the body.

Question. How does the title The Price of a Click reflect the main idea?
The title is a metaphor: a click looks free, yet it has a price. It condenses the main idea of the selection, that sharing news without checking it costs society trust and unity. The body develops this cost: false stories spread (paragraph 2), journalists lose credibility and communities divide (paragraph 5).
