Patterns of organisation

Every paragraph is built in a recognisable way: in time order, by causes and effects, by comparison… Name the pattern, then prove it with the words that signal it.

How it’s markedThe official split

0.5 for identification and 0.5 for explanation (2019). The official answer for June 2019: It is a chronological pattern of organization. The narrator lists the events that led her to discover her daughter’s illness. This pattern is illustrated by the use of time expressions such as “At first”, “One day”, “Then” and “later”. In July 2019 the answer was problem-solution, justified by the problem in the first sentences and the solution in the last.

See it doneWorked answers

Question. Identify the pattern of organisation of paragraph 8 of Wired Awake. Justify.
The pattern of organisation is chronological. The writer follows Karim over time, from his first attempt with the basket to its results. This is shown by the time expressions “for a month”, “the first three nights” and “by the second week”.

0.5 pattern, 0.5 justification with signal words.

Question. Identify the pattern of organisation of paragraph 3 of The Price of a Click. Justify.
The pattern is cause and effect. The writer explains why alarming posts spread: algorithms show what keeps us scrolling, and since anger and fear hold attention longer, these posts are pushed to the top. The link is signalled by “are designed to” and “Since…”.

0.5 pattern, 0.5 justification.

Texts: Wired Awake (LS) and The Price of a Click (SE), in the reading room.
